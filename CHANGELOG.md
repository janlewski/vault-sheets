# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First Vault Sheets release.

- Univer spreadsheet editing in the vault, including formulas, formatting, validation, filters, and comments.
- Clean-room xlsx and csv import and export.
- Sheet embeds, outgoing links, vault images, in-sheet charts, and save.
- No license-code settings. Calendar, Kanban, Dashboard, MCP, and chart embeds are not offered.

This line of development is an independent derivative of obsidian-sheet-plus at commit f4d3ea7. See NOTICE. It is not a continuation of that project's release numbers.
